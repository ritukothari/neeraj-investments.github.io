Disclosure

Important Disclosures

1. Source of Commission

All commissions are paid directly by the respective Asset Management Companies (AMCs) and are embedded within the Total Expense Ratio (TER) of the respective Mutual Fund Scheme as permitted under SEBI regulations.

No additional amount is charged separately to investors by NJ Financial Services towards such commissions.

2. Nature of Compensation

NJ Financial Services primarily receives trail commissions linked to the value of investments remaining invested in the schemes.

The actual commission received may vary across:

  • AMCs
  • Schemes
  • Product Categories
  • Investor Segments
  • Commission Slabs prescribed by AMCs

3. Conflict of Interest

NJ Financial Services endeavors to act in the best interests of investors while recommending suitable investment products.

The level of service, support, operational assistance, and investor engagement provided by NJ Financial Services is not influenced by the commission received from any AMC.

4. Investor Access to Commission Information

Investors may refer to their Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) issued by the Registrar or the AMC, which reflects the total commission paid to distributors for their investments.

5. Regular Plans

Investments facilitated through NJ Financial Services are executed only under the Regular Plan category of Mutual Fund schemes.

Investors should note that distributor commissions are payable only under Regular Plans and are not applicable under Direct Plans.

6. Classification of Schemes

For the purpose of this disclosure:

Equity-Oriented Schemes include:

  • Equity Mutual Funds
  • Equity-Oriented Hybrid Funds
  • Arbitrage Funds
  • Other schemes classified as equity-oriented under prevailing tax and regulatory provisions

Debt-Oriented Schemes include:

  • Debt Funds
  • Liquid Funds
  • Money Market Funds
  • Ultra Short Duration Funds
  • Corporate Bond Funds
  • Gilt Funds
  • Other non-equity-oriented schemes

7. Changes in Commission Structure

Commission structures are determined by the respective AMCs and may change from time to time without prior notice.

NJ Financial Services reserves the right to update this disclosure periodically to reflect changes in commission structures, regulatory requirements, or business practices.

Investors are advised to review the latest disclosure available on our website before making investment decisions.

8. Transaction Charges

NJ Financial Services has opted out of charging transaction charges under the provisions permitted by SEBI for Mutual Fund transactions.

Accordingly, transaction charges shall not be levied on eligible Mutual Fund purchases routed through NJ Financial Services unless otherwise required by future regulatory changes.

Regulatory Disclaimer

Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks. Please read all scheme-related documents carefully before investing.

Past performance may or may not be sustained in future and does not guarantee future returns.

The commission disclosure above is made solely in the interest of transparency and regulatory compliance and should not be construed as investment advice, solicitation, recommendation, or assurance of returns.
