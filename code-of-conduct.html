Code of Conduct

Neeraj Investments aims to conduct its financial-product distribution activities with integrity, fairness, transparency and due care.

We seek to provide clear information about products, risks, charges and relevant disclosures and to treat investors respectfully.

Investor information is handled responsibly and complaints or service concerns should be raised through our Contact page so they can be addressed promptly.

All applicable regulatory requirements and industry codes will be followed as relevant to the services offered.
