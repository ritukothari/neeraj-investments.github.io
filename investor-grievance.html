Investor Grievance Redressal

We are committed to providing timely and transparent resolution of investor queries and grievances. If you have a concern, please follow the escalation process below.

1

Level 1 – Customer Support

Investors should first contact NJ Financial Services with the details of their query or grievance. Please provide your name, contact details, relevant transaction or folio information and a clear description of the issue so that it can be reviewed promptly.

Level 2 – AMC Investor Relations / Grievance Redressal Officer

Escalate to Asset Management Company Investor Relations / Grievance Redressal Officer

Refer to the concerned AMC website for its Investor Relations and Grievance Redressal Officer contact details and escalation process.

3

Level 3 – SEBI SCORES

If you are not satisfied with the resolution provided, you may approach SEBI through the SCORES platform to submit and track your grievance. SCORES enables investors to lodge and track complaints against intermediaries regulated by SEBI.
